Three things it keeps三样都留住

Most converters give up at least one.别的工具往往至少丢掉一样。

01 · Cover frame封面帧

The frame you chose还是你选的那一帧

  • Aligned on the real video timeline, within half a frame按真实视频时间轴对齐，误差不到半帧
  • Missing timing is reported, never guessed时间信息缺失会直接提示，不拿中间帧凑数
02 · Quality画质

Video never re-encoded视频不重新编码

  • Original audio and video samples, just rewrapped原始音视频数据直接重新封装
  • Checked sample by sample before output输出前逐个样本校验
  • Apple HEIC → Android JPEG re-encodes the still: different format, and Android’s HEIC support is limited苹果 HEIC 转安卓 JPEG 时，静态图需重新编码：格式不同，且安卓对 HEIC 支持有限
03 · HDRHDR

Kept, or clearly flagged能留的都留，留不了就说明

  • Ultra HDR gain maps kept with their parametersUltra HDR 增益图连同参数保留
  • Unsupported HDR is flagged, never faked不支持的 HDR 会标出，不假装转好
  • Apple gain maps converted for Android may not be bit-exact苹果增益图转成安卓格式时，可能无法完全无损

FAQ常见问题

Can’t I just copy the file to my iPhone?直接拷到 iPhone 不行吗？

No — the formats differ, so it shows up as a still photo.不行。格式不同，拷过去只是一张静态图。

Will I lose quality?会掉画质吗？

Video and audio: never — they’re rewrapped and verified. Android → iPhone keeps the photo as is. iPhone HEIC → Android JPEG must re-encode the still (Android’s HEIC support is limited), and the HDR gain map may not carry over losslessly.视频和音频不会，只重新封装并校验。安卓转 iPhone，照片原样保留；iPhone 的 HEIC 转安卓 JPEG，静态图必须重新编码（安卓对 HEIC 支持有限），HDR 增益图也可能无法完全无损。

Are my photos uploaded?照片会上传吗？

No. Everything happens on your Mac.不会。全程在你的 Mac 本地完成。

Can I convert back to Android?能转回安卓吗？

Yes. Choose Apple → Android and the recipient’s phone brand.可以。选择「Apple → Android」和对方手机品牌即可。

Is it free?收费吗？

Yes, free and open source (GPL-3.0). Sponsoring on GitHub is welcome, never required.免费开源（GPL-3.0）。觉得好用，欢迎在 GitHub 赞助。
