Three things it refuses to lose

三件绝不妥协的事

Most converters quietly sacrifice one of these. MomentStays is built around keeping all three.

多数转换工具会悄悄牺牲其中之一。MomentStays 从设计上就是为了把三样都留住。

01 · Cover frame
01 · 封面帧

The cover frame stays yours

封面帧，还是你选的那一帧

The source cover timestamp is read and mapped through the real media timeline to the Live Photo timing marker, frame-aligned to within half a frame. Missing or ambiguous timing is reported — never silently replaced by the middle frame.

读取源文件的封面时间戳，映射到真实视频帧（对齐误差半帧以内）并写入实况照片标记。时间信息缺失或不明确时会明确报告，绝不偷偷用中间帧顶替。

02 · No re-encoding
02 · 不重新编码

Original streams, zero quality loss

原始音视频，画质零损失

Video and audio move as their original compressed samples — remuxed, never re-encoded — and verified sample by sample: hashes, sizes, counts, and presentation timestamps. Supported image paths keep the original JPEG/HEIC encoding.

视频和音频以原始压缩样本直接重新封装，绝不重新编码，并逐个样本校验哈希、大小、数量和时间戳。支持的图像路径保留原始 JPEG/HEIC 编码。

03 · HDR
03 · HDR

HDR preserved, honestly

HDR 保留，而且如实

Existing Ultra HDR gain maps are preserved with their parameters. Supported Apple/ISO gain data is converted through explicit models. Unsupported private HDR is reported as unsupported — never faked.

已有的 Ultra HDR 增益图连同参数原样保留；支持的 Apple/ISO 增益数据按明确模型转换；处理不了的私有 HDR 会如实报告，不装作转好了。

FAQ

Why not just copy the files to my iPhone?为什么不能把文件直接拷到 iPhone？

Android Motion Photos and Apple Live Photos are different container formats. A copied Motion Photo shows up as a still image. MomentStays converts the pairing metadata and cover timing so Photos recognizes it as a real Live Photo.

安卓动态照片和苹果实况照片是两种不同的封装格式，直接拷过去只会被识别成静态图。MomentStays 会转换配对元数据和封面时间，让「照片」把它认成真正的实况照片。

Does conversion reduce quality?转换会掉画质吗？

No. Video and audio are never re-encoded, and supported still-image paths keep their original encoding. Verification checks every sample before an output replaces anything.

不会。音视频从不重新编码，支持的静态图路径保留原始编码；输出在交付前逐个样本校验。

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?照片会被上传吗？

No. Conversion and verification run entirely on your Mac. iCloud downloads during a session are opt-in.

不会。转换与校验全部在你的 Mac 本地完成；iCloud 下载也仅在你主动开启的会话内进行。

Is it free?收费吗？

Yes — free and open source under GPL-3.0. If it saved your photos, you can support development via the Sponsor button on GitHub. Entirely optional.

免费开源（GPL-3.0)。如果它帮你留住了照片，可以通过 GitHub 的 Sponsor 按钮支持开发，完全自愿。

cd MomentStays
bash scripts/build_app.sh        # Intel
bash scripts/build_app.sh --universal
open dist/MomentStays.app

Current validation is on Intel / macOS 12; Apple Silicon validation is in progress. The app is ad-hoc signed (not notarized), so macOS may ask you to approve it once in Privacy & Security settings.
